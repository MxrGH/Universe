# Universe 1 — Final

Universe 1 is an all-in-one Geometry Dash 2.2091 Geode mod by MxrGH. This final release ships as Geode package v1.1.3.

The client includes local gameplay modifiers, Safe Mode, StartPos tools, object hitboxes, player-bounds overlay, movement trail and projection, FPS controls, Practice Mode replay tools, icon previews, local-only icon overrides, keybinds, themes, presets, and touch controls.

The Uni-1 AI page is experimental and generation is not operational: no trained adapter weights, deployed inference endpoint, or human-reviewed and playtested training corpus are included.

The universal package targets Windows x64, Android ARM64, and Android ARMv7. The mod has no additional Geode mod dependencies. Its Geometry Dash object-ID catalogue is bundled inside the package. Geode itself is required to load Geode mods.

CI compilation and package inspection do not replace installing and testing the mod in Geometry Dash on real devices.
