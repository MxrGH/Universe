# Universe

Universe is a Geode utility and practice toolkit for Geometry Dash 2.2091.

## Current development status

This repository is an early source scaffold. The settings and menu entry are present; gameplay hooks, the global click/tap audio hook, and the final hack-panel interface are still under development.

## Fair-play behavior

Safe Mode is intended to keep modified runs out of legitimate online records. Icon previews are local-only. Achievement and verification testing must remain local/offline and must not fabricate server-side achievements or leaderboard records.

## Build target

- Geometry Dash: 2.2091 (Windows)
- Geode: v6.0.0-alpha.1
